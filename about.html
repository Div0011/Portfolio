About Me

I am a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University (BBAU), Lucknow, entering my third year in July 2026. Specializing in bridging high-performance Full-Stack software engineering with cutting-edge AI systems, I write clean, secure, and robust applications.

The Engineering Philosophy I believe that code should be both structurally absolute and visually breathtaking. During my tenure as a developer at Mowglai.com, I architected cinematically immersive web designs driven by GSAP scrollytelling. By heavily optimizing graphic assets and refactoring WebGL rendering pipelines, I successfully achieved a 40% reduction in initial page load times, proving that aesthetic richness doesn't have to compromise performance.

Architecting Secure AI Systems My development model centers on FastAPI (Python), Next.js/React (TypeScript), and PostgreSQL. I have a deep passion for building resilient backends, specifically leveraging granular Row-Level Security (RLS) to enforce ironclad data isolation in multi-tenant SaaS environments (as implemented in GUARD-IT!). I also design advanced agentic loops utilizing Google Gemini and Hugging Face models, turning predictive analysis and background processing into reliable, self-improving workflows.

The Creative Interface Whether it is designing zero-knowledge storage vault pipelines (STORE-IT!) or building autonomous B2B crawling workflows (LeadGenius), I am constantly pursuing projects that challenge the boundaries of what is possible in web applications. I strive to make every interface feel responsive, alive, and mathematically precise, leaving a premium lasting impression.
